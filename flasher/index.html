Board firmware installer

Install the Network Survey board firmware on an ESP32 board straight from your browser. You only do this once: after this first install, the Network Survey iOS app updates the board over Bluetooth.

Before you start

  • Receive-only survey firmware. The board listens to Wi-Fi and Bluetooth and reports what it hears to the app. It never transmits Wi-Fi frames, never joins a network, and never sends active scans or deauthentication frames.
  • Only survey where you are allowed to. Rules on capturing wireless traffic differ by country and by site. You are responsible for following them.
  • Installing replaces everything on the board, including any previous firmware and Bluetooth pairings. Pair it with the app again afterward.

What you need

  • Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a desktop or laptop computer (Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS). Safari, Firefox and phone browsers cannot install firmware.
  • A USB cable that carries data. Many cables that come with chargers only carry power.
  • One of the boards below.

This browser cannot talk to USB serial devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

1 Connect the board

Plug the board into your computer with the USB cable. If the board has two USB ports, either one works; if the board is not found, try the other one.

2 Install the firmware

Press Install on your board, pick its serial port in the browser's list, then choose Install Network Survey Board and confirm. It takes about a minute. Keep the board plugged in until it says the installation is complete.

The board is not found, or the install fails to connect
  1. Try a different USB cable or port. Charge-only cables are the most common cause.
  2. Put the board in download mode: hold the BOOT button, press and release RESET (labeled RST or EN on some boards), then release BOOT. Press Install again. On the XIAO, hold BOOT while you plug in the cable instead.
  3. After an install in download mode, press RESET once so the board starts the new firmware.
  4. On Windows, boards with a USB to UART chip may need its driver (Silicon Labs CP210x or WCH CH34x).

3 Note the board's name

Every board has a short name such as NS-1A2B, made from its Bluetooth address. The app shows this name when you pair, so you can tell your boards apart. Close the installer window first, then:

You can also open Logs & Console in the installer and look for a line that says name NS-.

4 Pair it with the app

Open the Network Survey app on your iPhone, tap Boards at the top of the Survey screen, and pick your board's name under Nearby. A freshly installed board is ready to pair straight away. From now on, the app offers firmware updates and installs them over Bluetooth, so you will not need this page again for that board.
